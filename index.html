What Is Globalization?

The World Becomes More Connected.

Globalization is the growing connection of countries through the movement of goods, services, people, money, technology, and ideas.
▣

Goods

Products can be made in one country and bought in another.

↔

Services

Work and services can be offered to customers across borders.

◎

People

People travel, migrate, study, and work in different countries.

₱

Money

Businesses invest and financial activities move between countries.

✦

Ideas

Information, culture, Knowledge, and technology spread quickly.

Click a card to spotlight it
Source [1]: International Monetary Fund glossary — globalization describes the freer flow of ideas, people, goods, services, and capital that integrates economies and societies.

What Makes It Happen?

Four Forces Make the World Feel Smaller.

01

Internet + Communication

Messages, news, work, videos, and information can reach another country almost instantly.

02

Faster Transportation

Planes, ships, roads, and logistics make it easier to move people and products around the world.

03

International Trade

Countries buy and sell products and services from each other instead of relying only on local markets.

04

Technology + Business

Digital systems and international companies connect workers, customers, suppliers, and markets.

Click a card to spotlight it
Source [1][2] ↗: The IMF identifies technological progress, communication, trade, and cross-border flows as major parts of globalization.

The Different Sides of Globalization

It Is Not Only About Money.

↗

Economic

Trade, Jobs, companies, investments, products, and services connect different economies.

Example: Imported products and international companies
♫

Cultural

Music, movies, food, fashion, language, and traditions can spread between societies.

Example: Global music, films, food, and trends
⌁

Technological

Digital tools allow people to communicate, study, work, and exchange information globally.

Example: Social media and online meetings
◎

Social + Political

Countries and people cooperate on issues that cannot always be solved by one country alone.

Example: Migration and international Cooperation
Source [2] ↗: The IMF notes that globalization has economic as well as broader cultural, political, and environmental dimensions.

How Does It Work?

One Product Can Connect Many Places.

Ideas + Design

A product can be planned or designed by teams in different countries.

Materials + Parts

Raw materials and components may come from several places around the world.

Production + Shipping

Factories produce the item, while global transport moves it to different markets.

Software + Customers

Digital services, apps, support, and customers can also be spread across countries.

Our Agreed Definition
Group Two's Final Take
Globalization is the process of making the world more connected and interdependent as people, products, services, money, technology, culture, and ideas move across countries.

It can create opportunities and bring people closer, but its benefits and problems are not experienced equally. Understanding both sides helps us see globalization as something that affects everyday life, not only governments and large companies.
