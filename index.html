What Is Globalization?

The World Becomes More Connected.

Globalization is the growing connection of countries through the movement of goods, services, people, money, technology, and ideas.
▣

Goods

Products can be made in one country and bought in another.

↔

Services

Work and services can be offered to customers across borders.

◎

People

People travel, migrate, study, and work in different countries.

₱

Money

Businesses invest and financial activities move between countries.

✦

Ideas

Information, culture, Knowledge, and technology spread quickly.

What Makes It Happen?

Four Forces Make the World Feel Smaller.

01

Internet + Communication

Messages, news, work, videos, and information can reach another country almost instantly.

02

Faster Transportation

Planes, ships, roads, and logistics make it easier to move people and products around the world.

03

International Trade

Countries buy and sell products and services from each other instead of relying only on local markets.

04

Technology + Business

Digital systems and international companies connect workers, customers, suppliers, and markets.

Globalization Around Us

You Can See It in Everyday Filipino Life.

▣

Products

Phones, clothes, food, appliances, and other products may come from international brands or supply chains.

♫

Entertainment

Music, movies, games, anime, K-pop, and online trends move easily between countries.

⌨

Global Work

BPO, online services, freelancing, and remote work can connect Filipino workers with international clients.

✈

Migration

Filipinos may study, live, or work abroad while remaining connected to families at home.

◎

Communication

Social media, messaging, and video calls make international communication part of daily life.

✦

Learning

Students can access ideas, tutorials, research, and educational resources from around the world.
